FREE
  • Pro on your account for as long as you're in the program
  • Cloud sync, Coach, full analytics, PDF reports, app icons and accent colors
  • No limits on drills, guns or templates

CLIPS THAT POST

  • Results panel on your run video: drill, time, hit factor and hit zones
  • Live shot times synced to the gunshots in your video's sound (SG Timer runs)
  • Shown only in the last seconds, in the corners, so the shooter stays in frame
  • Full-quality export to Instagram, TikTok, YouTube or Photos

SHARE YOUR DRILLS

  • Build a drill with its diagram, then share it as a link or QR code
  • Your followers open it straight in the app and run it the way you do
  • Leaderboards on shared drills, so your audience can chase your time

EARLY ACCESS

  • New features in TestFlight before they reach the App Store
  • Tell us what to build next and talk to the developer directly

GET FEATURED

  • We share creators' clips on @allalphas.app (with your OK)

WHO IT'S FOR

HOW IT WORKS

THREE
STEPS.

01 EMAIL US Send your name, your handles and where you post, what you shoot, and a link to a few recent videos.
02 GET SET UP We reply by email. If it's a fit, we turn on Pro for the All Alphas account you sign in with.

Creators follow the Community Guidelines and the safety rules on the Safety page: safe gun handling, a safe and legal range and backstop, nothing for sale.
