STATEMENT OF FAITH

Serving our Lord and Savior, Jesus Christ, will always be our first priority.

Under no circumstances will we ever sacrifice this. Our faith will be shown by both words and actions as we grow.

Faith is the driver of every decision we make and we take deep responsibility in the stewardship He has entrusted us.

We encourage everyone to find and love Him, as He will always love you.
