All Alphas is a training log and timer. It doesn't teach firearm safety and it's no substitute for qualified instruction, range rules or the law where you live.

THE FOUR RULES OF FIREARM SAFETY · FOLLOW THEM AT ALL TIMES
1Treat every firearm as if it is loaded.
2Never point the muzzle at anything you aren't willing to destroy.
3Keep your finger off the trigger until your sights are on the target and you've decided to shoot.
4Be sure of your target and what is beyond it.
Dry fire: confirm the firearm is unloaded and keep all live ammunition out of the room. Aim only at a safe backstop. Obey range officers, range rules and match rules. Never look at your phone with a loaded firearm in your hand. Drills in the app are examples. Only attempt what you can do safely, at your own skill level.

You are solely responsible for how you handle firearms and ammunition. All Alphas accepts no liability for injury, death, property damage or legal consequences arising from using the app or from any drill, time or score it shows.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR PHONE

Drills, runs, scores, gear, ammo counts and target photos are stored on your device. We can't see them unless you turn on sync or share them.

WHAT WE COLLECT

If you create an account: your name and email from Sign in with Apple (or Apple's private relay address), and a display name. With Pro sync: a copy of your training data, stored encrypted with our hosting provider. With friends and teams: what you choose to share, such as runs, PBs, messages and range days. We also collect basic crash reports so we can fix bugs.

PERMISSIONS

Location is used only while the app is open, to find nearby ranges. It isn't stored or shared. Camera and photos are for target photos, Bluetooth for connecting a shot timer, and notifications for maintenance and ammo reminders. Every permission is optional.

WHAT WE DON'T DO

We don't sell your data, show ads, or use third-party advertising trackers. We don't track you across other apps or websites.

PROCESSORS

We use Apple for sign-in and payments, and a cloud database provider to host accounts and synced data. They process data only on our behalf.

RETENTION & DELETION

Delete your account in the app at Settings → Account → Delete account, or email us. Your account and cloud data are removed within 30 days. Data stored on your phone is deleted when you delete the app.

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data at any time. All Alphas isn't intended for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect data from children.
